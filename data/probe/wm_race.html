    Join Luang Prabang Half Marathon

    The Luang Prabang Half Marathon is an annual charity race dedicated to supporting the Lao Friends Hospital for Children. Runners from all over come together to participate in this meaningful event, promoting health, community spirit, and a great cause. The event offers various race categories, welcoming both seasoned athletes and casual participants.

    All proceeds go toward providing medical care for children in need, making every step count. Whether you're running for a personal challenge or to support a worthy cause, this race is an opportunity to make a difference while experiencing the beauty of Luang Prabang.

    Frequently asked questions

    When and where can I collect my BIB/race pack?

    keyboard_arrow_down

    The Race Entry Pack will be available on October 24 and 25 from 9am to 5pm at the Luang Prabang elementary school.

    What is the cancellation/refund policy for this event?

    keyboard_arrow_down

    After registration, there will be no fee refund for a Participant who does not eventually take part in the LPHM, for any reason whatsoever. The participant is strictly not allowed to transfer his or her race entry to another party.

    I’ve got some friends who want to come but don’t want to run. Is that ok?

    keyboard_arrow_down

    Please come spectate! In fact, even better, we have a special request – can you put them to work? Get them to support your fundraiser, put them in touch with us and get them to volunteer as marshals, route control, drink station attendants, cheerleaders with pom-poms, etc. It’ll be more fun for them that way, and we’ll appreciate the help.

    Will there be official timing and categories?

    keyboard_arrow_down

    There will be chip timing and the runners will be split up into age categories. Awards will be given for the overall male and female winner (top 3) in each distance.
